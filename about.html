About Us

Rooted and Rising Foundation is a non-profit organisation established in 2018, with the aim to support vulnerable children and families depending on grants as their only source of income through food assistance, clothing donations, education support and community development projects.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve the quality of life by creating opportunities for sustainable development and providing essential support for disadvantaged communities to help them grow and thrive.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a future where every individual has access to basic needs, education and equal opportunities.

Our Values

  • Community
  • Equality
  • Respect
  • Inclusion
  • Growth
